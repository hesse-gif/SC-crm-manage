What is Super Creator Partners?
Super Creator Partners is an always-on creator partnership program where creators make content by playing Supercent games and earn rewards based on performance.
Rewards run on two tracks below, and you can join both tracks at the same time.
Fixed CPM
Rewards based on views of your uploaded content
Creator Code
Rewards based on in-app purchases made using your unique creator code
 
💠 Fixed CPM Track
This track pays rewards based on the views your uploaded content receives.

Step 2. Payout
  1. Early each month, a payout notice is sent to participants whose reward amount — calculated based on performance accumulated through the end of the previous month — is $50 or more.
  2. Just submit the required documents as guided. Please note that we’ll also ask you to grant scp@supercent.io viewer access to your YouTube channel to verify views and audience data. (This is read-only access, and you can revoke it anytime under YouTube Studio Settings → Permissions.)

💰 Rewards
  • Calculated as total views in the 7 days after upload × the fixed CPM for your country tier.
  • The fixed CPM is based on your channel’s main audience country over the past 28 days.
  • The minimum payout amount is $50. If you don’t reach this amount, no payout occurs and the balance rolls over to the following month.

🎬 Content Guide
Content that doesn’t meet the criteria below may be excluded from rewards.
You can download sample videos for content creation here.
Basic Rules
Item Requirement
Content length At least 20 seconds
Game name State the game name accurately
Link Include the target game’s store link; other game links are not allowed
Hashtags #SuperCreatorPartners #Supercent #GameName
Ad disclosure Clearly disclose program participation and sponsorship in the video or description
Visibility Videos must remain public
Visual Requirements
Item Requirement
Using provided sample footage If you use footage we provide, it must be used in a transformative (secondary-creation) form
Face visibility Recommended that your face fill at least 25% of the frame
Captions Captions are required
Video/audio quality Video and audio must be clear and legible
Prohibited Actions
Item What’s prohibited
Manipulated engagement Artificially inflating views, likes, comments, or followers. Views obtained abnormally (paid views, bot views, etc.) are excluded from the count
Repeated uploads Uploading the same video repeatedly
Copyright infringement Unauthorized use of others’ content, such as unapproved movie/TV/celebrity clips
Right of publicity violations Including a third party’s face, voice, or personal information without consent
Hate speech / misinformation Discriminatory language, exaggerated claims, or misleading statements
Missing ad disclosure Intentionally hiding program participation or the reward structure
Damaging brand image Avoid negative content about the game
Platform policy violations Violating YouTube’s or any platform’s operating, advertising, or community guidelines; sexual/violent or otherwise inappropriate content
